MY AWESOME TEXT

Once dominated by homegrown players, the National Basketball Association is growing more international. This season, a record-breaking 135 international players made it to the league.

"How we grow our kids to play basketball is flawed. We don't play five-on-five enough no more, pickup games, going to the park, getting knocked down, getting up… Everybody’s got a trainer, we babied.

In Europe and elsewhere kids struggle to get to where they need to get to. When they're young, they’re not getting the NIL bag like we’re getting over here. It's a hunger disparity.

I don't see the hunger like we’re seeing from a lot of these international players who’re still coming up the same way they've been coming up the whole 100 years we've been playing this game. But things have changed in America.

American players are not the cream of the crop no more. We have the majority, but at the top — we’re not there. Our generation is gone, and we don't feature the top players no more — the last seven MVPs have been international. "

— Paul Pierce, Basketball Hall of Fame member
